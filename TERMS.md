## 7. Machines: your risk, your safety duties

Several apps control real machines: 3D printers (filament and resin), cutting machines,
3D scanners, turntables and smart plugs. All of these are optional. You use the apps with
any machine at your own risk. In particular:

- **Never leave a heating or cutting machine unattended** while it is running, heating or
  able to start. 3D printers have heaters that can start fires; cutters have sharp blades.
- **A smart plug can switch on whatever is plugged into it.** Don't switch a plug that
  powers a heater, printer or other risky equipment unless you are there.
- **Follow the maker's safety instructions** for every machine and material: ventilation
  and gloves for resin, eye safety for scanners, guards and blade care for cutters, safe
  temperatures and limits for printers.
- **Check what you send.** Look over G-code, cut files and settings before starting a job.
  The apps include limits and confirmations, but they can't know every machine or setup.
- **Keep a way to stop the machine** that doesn't depend on the app: its own stop button
  or power switch.
- **Firmware changes are your choice.** Installing other firmware (such as PowerCut's
  firmware for compatible cutters) may void the machine's warranty and could make it
  unusable. Do it only if you accept that.
